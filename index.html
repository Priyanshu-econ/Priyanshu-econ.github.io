PhD Scholar in Economics, Pandit Deendayal Energy University

Priyanshu Chavda

I am an applied economist working in energy and environmental economics. I enjoy starting from the data: building it, bringing the right econometric model to it, and then doing the part I find most rewarding, which is interpreting what the results say about the economy and the environment.

My toolkit is applied econometrics, mostly time series and panel data methods, put to work on questions of energy use, emissions, and the economics of the transition to cleaner energy.
