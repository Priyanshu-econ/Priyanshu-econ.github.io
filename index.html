I study how energy policy, fiscal choices, and trade shape environmental outcomes and the transition to renewable energy, using time series and panel data methods.

I am a PhD scholar in the Department of Economics at the School of Liberal Studies, Pandit Deendayal Energy University (PDEU). Before the PhD, I completed an M.A. in Economics at PDEU, graduating as gold medalist, and a B.A. in Economics at L.D. Arts College, Gujarat University.

My work looks at questions such as whether fossil fuel subsidies hold back renewable energy, how green trade affects India's ecological footprint, and how energy security differs across Indian states. I hope to contribute both to academic research and to policy advice on sustainable development.
